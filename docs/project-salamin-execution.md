# Project SALAMIN Execution Plan

The job is not to launch a Facebook Page or convince a known creator to join us. The job is to make one 60-second SALAMIN challenge work with real Filipinos, collect approved anonymous results from 30 different adults, and bring the footage and honest results into the UNESCO submission.

The work has to happen in this order:

> *One complete challenge –\> 10 different adults complete dry tests –\> lock the format –\> record participants until 30 adults approve anonymous result use –\> publish at least three approved videos –\> pilot report and pitch*

If the challenge is confusing, the Creator Pack only helps us repeat the confusion. If we wait for an outside creator, we may never film. If we publish only the most dramatic reactions, we may have content but no honest pilot evidence.

Prove the interaction first. Let the brand and distribution follow.

No names are assigned here yet, but every workstream gets one owner and one deadline. One owner does not mean one person does all the work. It means one person makes sure the work gets finished.

## The Finish Line

Our internal deadline is **August 15, 2026**, one day before the official UNESCO deadline.

By then, we need the smallest body of work that proves SALAMIN was actually run:

* **A working challenge:** one tested Green and Pink post set, one Creator Pack, and unfilmed dry tests with 10 different adults.  
* **A completed pilot:** one filming team and location, approved anonymous results from 30 different adults, and at least three finished public videos made only with participant approval. Our own team can run it.  
* **A real submission:** one short pilot report, the final proposal, and a three-minute pitch using our footage and results.

The stretch target is three creators and approved anonymous results from 100 different adults. That number is irrelevant until the first 30 work.

## The Decisions We Are Making

We are capable of building software. That is not a reason to turn SALAMIN into a software project. The pilot only needs a shared folder, a design tool, a form or spreadsheet, and the interviewer's existing Facebook account. No custom app, participant account, or shared SALAMIN login is required. This is all to make the interaction raw and get realistic inputs, something hard to get from an app alone. 

We follow three rules:

1. Finish and test one complete challenge before polishing the brand or producing more assets.  
2. Approach outside creators, but never let the pilot wait for them. Keep one final version of every file so whoever films receives the same instructions.  
3. Put every participant who approves anonymous data use into the tracker, not only the reactions worth publishing.

## Execution Order

### Phase 1: Build The First Complete Challenge

**Target: July 31-August 2**

Build only what is needed to run SALAMIN once:

* **The interaction:** matched Green and Pink post images, exact Facebook captions, and the 60-second script.  
* **The lesson:** the mirror reveal and SALAMIN Check screens.  
* **The operation:** the recording, data, and publishing consent flow plus a first participant tally.

These are not separate assets yet. They are one working challenge. Run the full thing inside the team and check the timer, phone flow, questions, reveal, and tally together.

**Done when:** someone can open two private Facebook posts, run the challenge in around 60 seconds, explain the reveal, and record the result without inventing anything on the spot.

### Phase 2: Run 10 Dry Tests

**Target: August 2-3**

Run the challenge with 10 Filipino adults. Do not film for publication yet. The only job of these tests is to find out whether a stranger can reach the SALAMIN moment without us explaining the idea around it.

After every run, record:

* **Friction:** where the participant became confused, whether the questions sounded natural, and how long the interaction took.  
* **Safety:** whether the parties and claim felt fictional or could be mistaken for real information.  
* **Understanding:** whether they saw that both posts lacked proof and could explain what SALAMIN was teaching.

Fix the wording after the first five people. Test the revised version with the next five.

**Done when:** the interaction fits around 60 seconds, at least eight of the 10 participants understand the mirror, and nobody leaves believing the fictional claim was real. Eight out of 10 is not proof that SALAMIN works across the Philippines. It is our gate for deciding whether the wording is clear enough to film.

### Phase 3: Prepare The Filming Team

**Target: August 4-5**

Finish the Creator Pack and Data Tracker. The pack does not exist to prove that we have a creator partner. It exists so the challenge stays consistent whether an outside creator or our own team runs it.

Approach possible creators and host organizations, but do not make the pilot depend on them.

By August 5, choose one filming route:

* **Partner route:** An outside creator runs the challenge with our support. A host organization may provide participants and a location.  
* **Team route:** One or more SALAMIN team members act as the interviewer and filming crew. We find the participants and location ourselves.

Either route uses the same script, safety rules, consent flow, and tracker. The person running the challenge must know how to:

* **Set up:** publish both posts using the Facebook "Only Me" audience and verify that nobody else can see them.  
* **Run:** ask without guiding the answer, show the complete mirror, and debrief without mocking the participant.  
* **Close:** handle recording, data, and publishing consent separately; update the private tally; and publish the complete lesson instead of an embarrassing reaction alone.

Rehearse with the actual interviewer before approaching participants.

**Done when:** the filming route, interviewer, location, and first filming schedule are confirmed. An outside creator or host is useful, but not required.

### Phase 4: Run The Pilot

**Target: August 6-10**

Keep interviewing different adults until 30 have completed the full 60-second challenge while recorded with prior consent and allowed their anonymous answers to enter the tracker. This may require approaching more than 30 people.

The number 30 refers to participants in the private tally. It does not mean 30 creators or 30 published videos. If we secure a creator or host organization, we run the pilot with them. If not, the SALAMIN team records the interviews directly in a safe location where recording is allowed.

The team monitors the first runs before letting the format repeat. Someone must interview, someone must film, and someone must update the tracker. The same people can switch jobs between runs.

During the pilot:

* **Before:** confirm that the participant is at least 18 without collecting their birthdate, get recording permission, and keep both posts set to "Only Me."  
* **During:** give the complete reveal and debrief, ask separately if anonymous answers may enter the tally, and stop if the participant becomes uncomfortable.  
* **After:** ask separately if the footage may be published, delete footage without approval, and record every approved anonymous result even when the video is boring.

The videos and tracker do different jobs. The videos make SALAMIN watchable. The tracker tells us what happened across every approved run.

Publish at least three finished videos using footage from participants who separately approve publication. Every public video must contain the mirror, the MIL lesson, and a clear disclaimer that the parties, number, and claim were created for SALAMIN.

**Done when:** the tracker contains approved anonymous results from 30 different adults, at least three finished videos are public, and the team has enough footage and participant reflections for the UNESCO pitch.

### Phase 5: Turn The Pilot Into Evidence

**Target: August 11-15**

Close the pilot before writing the final submission. Clean the tracker, combine the results, and separate what happened from what we merely think it means.

The second post is a mirror, not a clean scientific measurement. A participant may change their answer because they recognized the repeated claim, realized they were being tested, or wanted to look consistent on camera. We can report the answers and reflections from those 30 adults. We cannot claim that political identity caused any change.

Produce:

* The short Pilot Report.  
* The final UNESCO proposal and three-minute pitch using real SALAMIN footage.  
* A final folder containing every submitted file and a backup of the submission receipt.

**Done when:** the report and pitch explain the problem, show the 60-second challenge, present honest pilot results, state the limitations, and show how another creator or organization can repeat it.

## Build Specs

### 1\. Post Assets

The first set contains:

* One matched Green and Pink Facebook post pair with exact private captions.  
* One mirror reveal showing both posts together.  
* One SALAMIN Check screen for the end of the video.

The Green and Pink versions must use the same claim, number, layout, image quality, and missing proof. Only the party identity and color change.

The parties and claim must be fictional. Do not use a real politician, candidate, election promise, disaster, public emergency, or live political issue.

**Asset check:**

* The claim is readable on a phone and neither post contains a source, project list, or evidence.  
* Neither version looks more credible than the other.  
* The reveal says both posts were created for SALAMIN, and the final exports work inside actual private Facebook posts.

### 2\. Creator Pack

The pack should be one small folder, not a pile of separate explanations. It is written for whoever runs the challenge, including our own team.

It contains:

* **Set up and run:** Start Here, private post setup, and the exact 60-second script.  
* **Film safely:** the consent flow, filming guide, and debrief guide.  
* **Publish and record:** editing rules, standard caption and disclaimer, comment guide, and Data Tracker.

The working script follows this flow:

1. **0-23 seconds:** Ask "Green, Pink, neither, or prefer not to answer?" Then show the aligned post and ask, "Believe it, check it first, or share it? Why?"  
2. **23-50 seconds:** Show the opposition post and ask the same question. Then reveal: "Both posts make the same claim. Both show no proof. Only the side changed. Did changing the side change your answer?"  
3. **50-60 seconds:** End with, "Next time a political post supports your side, ask: Nasaan ang patunay?"

Consent happens before the timer. Publication approval happens after the reveal.

### 3\. Data Tracker

Use one private form or spreadsheet. Create a random Run ID for each participant. Do not collect names, Facebook accounts, phone numbers, emails, or exact birthdates.

Record:

* **Run details:** Run ID, date, interviewer, general location, and confirmation that the participant is 18 or older.  
* **Responses:** chosen side, first post shown, both answers, whether they changed, missing proof noticed, and whether the mirror was understood.  
* **Permissions:** approval to include anonymous answers, approval to publish footage, and the public video link when applicable.

The tracker covers all completed and approved runs. Published videos are content. They are not the dataset. Mixing those two would reward the most dramatic reactions and erase everyone who judged both posts carefully.

### 4\. Public Brand

Create a Project SALAMIN Facebook Page with:

* A simple profile image, cover, and one-sentence description.  
* A pinned explainer with the standard fictional-content disclaimer.  
* A place to share completed videos, combined results, and future creator calls.

Do not publish the unsupported Green and Pink posts on the SALAMIN Page. They exist only as private posts on creator accounts. The public content is always the completed challenge with the reveal.

The Page makes the work visible. It does not make the pilot real. It must not delay the dry tests or filming.

### 5\. Pilot Report

Keep the report short. It needs:

* **What we ran:** the challenge, participant count, filming team, and any outside creator or host involved.  
* **What happened:** Green and Pink answers, missing proof noticed, participant reflections, and public video performance.  
* **What we learned:** filming difficulty, changes made, what the results may suggest, what they cannot prove, and what the next run should improve.

The report should let a reviewer see both the result and where our evidence stops. Do not claim that those 30 adults represent the Philippines. SALAMIN is a public learning challenge with exploratory data, not a controlled academic experiment.

## First Team Meeting

The first meeting is not for explaining the idea again. It only needs to produce three decisions:

1. Approve the first fictional claim and create the shared workspace.  
2. Put one owner beside every row, then confirm the dry-test participants, first interviewer, filming location, and optional partners to approach.

The first useful proof is one stranger experiencing the mirror clearly and safely. Everything else is noise.
